Return Policy

Return Window
Customers may return eligible items within 30 days of the delivery date.

Condition Requirements
Items must be returned unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with all original tags attached. Items that show signs of use or are missing original packaging will not be accepted.

Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Sale and clearance items (all sales are final)
- Digital downloads and software licences
- Perishable goods
- Customised or personalised items

Return Shipping
Customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping. We recommend using a tracked service, as we cannot be responsible for items lost in transit.

Refund Processing
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

How to Initiate a Return
To start a return, email support@store.com with your order number and the reason for your return. Our support team will provide return instructions.

The support agent should rely only on the policy information contained in this document. If a customer's question requires a policy detail not specified here, the agent should not invent the missing detail and should escalate.
