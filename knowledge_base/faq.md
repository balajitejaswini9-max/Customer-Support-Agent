# Frequently Asked Questions

## How to Contact Support
Customers can reach our support team by email at support@store.com or through the live chat widget on our website. Business hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM EST.

## Order Processing Time
Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed. A confirmation email with order details is sent once the order has been processed.

## Cancellation Policy
Orders cannot be cancelled after they have been placed and confirmed. If you contact us immediately after placing your order, we will attempt to cancel before processing begins, but we cannot guarantee this. For urgent cancellation requests, email support@store.com as soon as possible.

## Payment Methods
We accept the following payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cheques, bank transfers, or cryptocurrency.

## General Support
If the knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer a customer question, the support agent should escalate rather than invent an answer.
