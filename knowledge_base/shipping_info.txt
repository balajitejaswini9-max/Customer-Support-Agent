Shipping Information

Domestic Shipping Options
- Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days
- Express Shipping: 2–3 business days
- Overnight Shipping: next business day (orders placed before 2 PM EST)

Shipping Costs
- Standard shipping is free on orders over $50.
- Orders under $50 incur a flat $5.99 standard shipping fee.
- Express shipping costs $12.99 per order.
- Overnight shipping costs $24.99 per order.

International Shipping
We ship to select international destinations. International orders typically arrive within 10–14 business days. Additional customs fees, duties, and taxes may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient. Not all products are available for international shipping.

Order Tracking
Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update in the carrier's system after shipment.

The support agent should answer only from shipping information contained in this document. If a requested shipping detail is not specified, the agent should escalate rather than guess.
